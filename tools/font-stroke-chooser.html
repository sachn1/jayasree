Font stroke chooser

For each letter, choose which designer strokes make up its font-based version. Every stroke of every font is a card of its own. Choices are saved in this browser; export them to tools/font-stroke-choices.ml.json, then run build_font_strokes.py build.

No server? Choose these three files from the repo (select them together): js/src/glyph-data.json, js/src/font-strokes.ml.json, and optionally js/src/stroke-data.json (to show today's handwriting for comparison)

Click a card (or press its number) to add that stroke to the letter, and again to remove it. The letter is drawn in the order you click; use ◀ ▶ under "Your letter" to change it, and ⇄ to draw a stroke from its other end. The grey shape is the Manjari outline every font is centred on. "Ships today" is the recorded handwriting, shown for reference only. The font strokes are from SMC's Chilanka, Manjari and Nupuram fonts (SIL Open Font License; see LICENSE-DATA).
